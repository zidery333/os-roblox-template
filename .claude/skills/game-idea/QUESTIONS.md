# Questions every idea answers

Every idea pitched in chat answers each question below in one short line. The idea card (step 5 of `SKILL.md`)
answers them in full, for the one idea the person wants to build. The person can change, add or cut any question.
When their answer to an idea teaches something, it becomes a new or changed question here, never a stored idea.

| # | Question | Why it's asked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | **Does it stand out?** The name and one line alone: would someone stop scrolling for it? Ask this first; an idea that doesn't stand out isn't pitched. | Sets of ideas can answer every other question and still be forgettable. |
| 1 | **Why would it be fun?** In this game's own terms: what is fun about the thing you do most, every minute of a whole round? | Ideas pieced together from other games feel borrowed, and a round that is one dull action ("a whole round just shoving coal?") is boring. |
| 2 | **Why would someone click on it?** The one picture that shows it truthfully, and the five-second moment a stranger would clip. | Roblox Home needs a thumbnail; clips bring first players. |
| 3 | **What does the player do in the first minute,** with no tutorial? The one verb (pull, steal, grow). | Roblox counts players who leave before 60 seconds against a game (Discovery doc, 2026-11-14). |
| 4 | **Is it repeatable?** What is new each time you play? | A game that plays the same every round gets old fast. |
| 5 | **Why would someone keep playing?** Why come back tomorrow, what is the goal in week 3, is progress saved, and is it more than bigger numbers for money? The pull must come from the main action itself, not something added on. | Roblox measures returns on day 1, days 2-7 and days 8-28. No honest week-3 goal: "short ride", a tie-breaker, not a kill. |
| 6 | **Does it work on a phone?** Thumbs, camera, lag. | Most Roblox players are on phones. |
| 7 | **Where do the players live?** Their home or base. | A place to come back to gives the game a centre. |
| 8 | **Alone and in a group?** What one player does, what friends do, and what bots do in empty places. | Every game made here must work for one player and for a group. |
| 9 | **Is it its own game?** The closest game on Roblox (search it), and what is different in what the player does, not only the look. | Roblox stops pushing look-alikes. |
| 10 | **Would players aged 16+ like it?** | New games are shown first only to age-checked players aged 16+ (Kids and Select doc, 2026-11-14). |
| 11 | **What is the regular new thing,** every 2 to 4 weeks, and how many models does one drop need? | Keeps players coming back. Models only get made when the person starts a model chat. |
| 12 | **What might a player pay for?** A thing, not a price. | Prices are the person's. |

## Rules for the answers

- Mark each answer the person did not say "(pitch)".
- The name is a working name. The picture is one sentence: draw nothing, pick no look, colour or price.
- An answer that is a guess says so. "I don't know yet" beats a confident guess.
