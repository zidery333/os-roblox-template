# Marketing a Roblox game: working method (as of 2026-11-15)

## The short version
- Growth comes from Home's "Recommended for You" (RFY). It ranks a game only on players who clicked it there. Ads, friends and videos only get the game tested.
- A new game is locked to age-checked 16+ players until it logs 250 highly engaged plays within 60 days.
- Do the free things first: custom thumbnail (required), plain gameplay video, three thumbnails, an event per update.
- Ads are a small paid seed and a thumbnail test. They do not buy ranking.
- Roblox publishes no pass marks. Compare the game with itself week on week and with the dashboard percentiles.

## Steps
1. **Set up the account once.** Age check, 2FA, verified email. Grant the Studio MCP its Open Cloud scopes (thumbnails, assets, analytics read, events). *Done when:* the AI can pull one analytics query. Confidence: high on requirements, low on the API path (docs read, never called).
2. **Fix rating and audience at the idea stage.** Aim for Minimal, Mild or Moderate. Avoid free-form creation tools, hangout spaces and heavy realistic blood. Use R15. The hook must appeal to players 16 and older. Name the five-second moment a stranger would clip. *Done when:* the AI's draft of the ratings questionnaire raises no flag. High.
3. **Build the first session for a cold stranger in an empty server.** Log a funnel step at each onboarding stage. Give a reason to return tomorrow and in weeks 2-4. Make joining friends easy. Skip AFK padding. *Done when:* two or three early testers (10 invites allowed before release) reach the core loop unaided. High on the signals. "Core action within 30 seconds" is our own rule of thumb.
4. **Ship the template code.** Notification opt-in shown after a high moment. Friend referral reward. Join-source logging. A text scan for off-platform pointers. Purchases wait for Gate 4 (one or two at a fixed price, tied to progress). *Done when:* the scan is clean. High on rules, medium on monetisation timing.
5. **Name and page.** Short, unique name, never changed. First description sentence says what you do. Honest genre. Update news goes in Promotional Text (beta), not the title. Best 16:9 image first in the gallery, because search shows it. *Done when:* a name-clash check is done and the person has picked. High.
6. **Thumbnails.** Three clearly different 16:9 concepts from real engine captures, all active. Keep words off the bottom edge. Set the square icon too. Upload days early: everything is moderated. Review only at updates. *Done when:* three are live. High that one is required. Low on lift size: Roblox's +8.5% is undated and measured on a retired metric.
7. **Gameplay video.** Real play, core loop clear in the first seconds, no text, voice-over, lyrics or cinematic shots. One careful upload. *Done when:* it is approved. Medium: +30% and +41% RFY plays, but from two games Roblox picked.
8. **Publish through the gate.** A 16+ release lists no fee; the full questionnaire and the fee wait for Gate 4. Publish into a weekend with update one already built. Re-read the Kids and Select page at every launch. *Done when:* the Audience Reach tab counts toward 250. High.
9. **Seed players, cheapest first.** Early testers, friends through a tracked share link, then a Roblox Community. One share link per channel. *Done when:* the Home Recommendations tab shows impressions. Medium: Roblox says the bar is "very low" and gives no number. Plan for weeks.
10. **One ad test, only if Home stays at zero after a week or two.** Plays objective, no targeting, 5 credits a day for 3 days, lifetime budget, fixed end date, auto-reload off, three thumbnails, no pausing. *Done when:* plays per credit for each thumbnail is read two days after it ends. Trust only gaps of several times. Low: Roblox says judge after 7-10 days, so this reading is rough.
11. **Free placements.** Nominate for Standout Games if the mechanic or look is new. One event per real update, created 7 days ahead with featuring ticked, only after the build passes. One 60-character announcement (one allowed every 3 days). Medium.
12. **Weekly read.** The AI pulls the Home cohort only: play-through rate, bounce under 60 s and at 61-180 s, play days for Day 1, 2-7 and 8-28, playtime. The weakest against the dashboard's P10/P50/P90 is the next build job. One change per update. Ignore RFY dips during ad runs. High on what to read, low on tiny samples.
13. **Update rhythm.** Every 2-4 weeks, with a scripted event that runs itself at a fixed weekend hour and a claim window of days. Repost the gameplay cut on one outside channel with its own share link. Low to medium.

## What needs the person themselves
Times are the checker's estimates, not sourced.
- One-time set-up and MCP scopes: 30-40 minutes. Their age decides card payment (18+) and ID versus face estimate. Ask them.
- Per game: File > Publish at 16+ (no fee; the full questionnaire and fee wait for Gate 4) (the MCP never publishes): 15-20 minutes.
- Pick the name and the thumbnails: 5 minutes each.
- Record 3-5 minutes of real play and upload the cut: 30-45 minutes the first time.
- Watch two or three testers play without helping: 30 minutes.
- Real phone test per release: 10-15 minutes.
- Share links (2 minutes each), notification texts and referral banner (5 minutes each), prices (5 minutes).
- Ad credit and the spend decision: 20-30 minutes. Ads need the person's own account and cost real money. Robux turned into credits never come back. Fifteen credits is about 3,945 Robux at the 2025 rate.
- Standout form: 15-20 minutes. Community: 100 Robux, 5 minutes.
- A written stop rule for when a game goes on hold. No source gives one.

## Never do
- Judge the game on blended numbers. Ad players never count in ranking and behave worse.
- Buy bots, visits, likes or "CCU boosts". Roblox excludes them and can suspend the account.
- Use "FREE", Robux or giveaway wording, lookalike names, or thumbnails showing what the game lacks. Exposure is cut and recovery takes days to weeks.
- Put a Discord invite, handle, URL or QR code in the game. Only the page's Social Links are allowed.
- Run contests with Robux or cash prizes. Banned.
- Sell random items for Robux in version one. They need exact odds and a country check.
- Leave an ad on "run continuously". One developer lost 75,744 Robux that way.
- Use the Engagement or Earnings objectives, expedited review (50,000 Robux), the featured tile ($5,000) or paid creators. Studio budgets.
- Follow notes about QPTR, a 7-day window or keyword search ads. All retired.
- Write forum figures such as "50-100 CCU" down as rules. No staff confirmed any.
- Build on someone else's meme character or brand. One hit had to pull a character.

## Where good sources disagree
- **Ads and Home.** Roblox says ads never change rank. Several developers report Home traffic falling while ads ran. Roblox has not answered them.
- **Cold start.** Roblox says new games are tested daily. Developers report zero Home impressions for one to three weeks.
- **Cost per play.** Roblox's last figure is $0.0073 (November 2025, now historical). Developers in 2026 report $0.002 to $0.075.
- **Short video.** Roblox and Naavik credit YouTube and TikTok. The only small-developer numbers found show zero joins, from one developer.
- **Fee refund.** One doc says 90 days. Another says after holding 25 highly engaged players for 60 days.

## What nobody knows yet
- Whether Home recommends a game at all during the 16+ trial, and how big that audience is.
- The real "very low minimum", and what makes a player "highly engaged".
- Any solo launch after June 2026 with numbers. Every success story is from 2025.
- The promo-code rule. Roblox has one; the page could not be opened.
- Whether a public test weekend starts the 28-day Up-and-Coming clock.
- How many players are needed before Home numbers mean anything.
- Today's ad credit price, and whether Plays ads count toward the 250.
- What hit makers spent or did in week one.
