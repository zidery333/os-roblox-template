# Market a game

Get one of the person's games found on Roblox and growing. Good: strangers arrive from Home, the Home numbers rise
week on week, and no Robux moved without the person's yes. Step 2 is fixed at the idea stage; the rest starts once Gate 1 is
passed (the page, thumbnails and publishing are Gate 2's work). Every step is new to this folder until "What we tried" says otherwise.

Works the same on Mac and Windows. On Windows, `./os` runs in the Linux (WSL) terminal, and Roblox Studio and the
Roblox website run on Windows as normal.

## Before you start
- Read the About me note in `Notes/`, if there is one. The person's rules there beat this file. Ask them one thing at
  a time.
- Read "What we tried" in `.claude/skills/market-game/REFERENCE.md` (detail, sources).
- Roblox's own rules in REFERENCE.md and the person's rules win every clash.
- Numbers here were read on 2026-11-15. Any number over a month old: open its source again first. Rules change often.
- Hold four facts:
  - Growth comes from Home's "Recommended for You". It ranks a game only on players who clicked it there.
  - Ads, friends and videos only get the game tested. Ad players never count in ranking.
  - A new game reaches only age-checked players of 16 and over until it logs 250 highly engaged plays in 60 days.
    Re-read the Kids and Select page at each launch: a cut to 100 was promised, and two pages differ on the fee refund.
  - Roblox publishes no pass marks. Compare the game with itself week on week and with its dashboard band.

## Steps
Start at the step asked for, or the first not done on this game. **The person** does the parts marked for them, and
nothing replaces them; the minutes are the checker's estimates, not sourced. Steps 3 and 4 are build work: see Hand
over. A 16+ release lists no fee (2026-11-15). The full questionnaire, the fee and purchases come at the plan's
Gate 4. Re-read the Publish page before the person pays anything: two Roblox pages disagreed on the fee.
1. **Account, once.** Age check, 2FA, verified email. The person grants the Studio MCP its Open Cloud scopes:
   thumbnails, assets, analytics read, events. **The person, 30-40 min.** Ask if they are 18 or over: it decides
   card payment. Ask who puts pictures and events live: them by hand, or the AI after their yes. Done when: the AI
   pulls one analytics query. Unsure: read in docs, never called. If it fails, the person reads numbers off the
   dashboard.
2. **Rating and audience, at the idea stage.** Aim for Minimal, Mild or Moderate. No free-form creation
   tools, hangout spaces or heavy realistic blood. Use R15. The hook must pull players 16 and older. Name the
   five-second moment a stranger would clip. Done when: the AI's draft ratings questionnaire raises no flag.
3. **First session, for a cold stranger in an empty server.** Log a funnel step at each onboarding stage.
   A reason to return tomorrow and in weeks 2-4. Easy friend joining. No AFK padding. **The person, 15 min each:**
   watch two or three early testers (10 invites allowed), no helping. Done when: they reach the core loop unaided.
4. **Template code.** Notification opt-in after a high moment, friend referral reward, join-source logging,
   a text scan for off-platform pointers. Purchases wait for Gate 4: 1-2 at a fixed price, tied to progress,
   hidden in the first session. **The person, 5 min each:** notification texts, referral banner. Done when: clean
   scan.
5. **Name and page.** Short, unique name, never changed. First description sentence says what you do.
   Honest genre. Update news goes in Promotional Text (beta), not the title. Best 16:9 picture first in
   the gallery. **The person, 5 min:** picks the name. Done when: a name-clash check is done and they have picked.
6. **Thumbnails.** Home needs one custom thumbnail. Make three clearly different 16:9 ideas (1920x1080) from
   real captures of the game (taken in a /build-game step), all active, plus the square icon. No words on the bottom
   edge. Upload days early: all are moderated. **The person, 5 min:** picks them. Done when: three are live. Unsure
   how much they lift plays.
7. **Gameplay video.** Real play, core loop clear in the first seconds. No text, voice-over, lyrics or cinematic
   shots. One careful upload: 3 a month, rejections count. **The person, 30-45 min** the first time: records 3-5
   minutes, the AI cuts it, they upload. Done when: it is approved. Unsure of the lift: the proof is two games Roblox
   picked.
8. **Publish to 16+ (the plan's Gate 2).** **The person, 15-20 min:** the 16+ questions and File > Publish, into a
   weekend with update one already built. The full ratings questionnaire and the fee (1,000 Robux, or
   Plus/Premium for 2 months) come with all ages, at Gate 4. **The person, 10-15 min:** real phone test. No kill
   line in the plan? Ask them for one. Done when: Audience Reach counts toward 250.
9. **Seed players, cheapest first.** Early testers, then friends through a tracked share link, then a
   Roblox Community (100 Robux, **the person, 5 min**). One link per channel: **the person, 2 min each**, they send
   them. Done when: the Home Recommendations tab shows impressions. Plan for weeks: Roblox gives no number.
10. **One ad test, only if Home stays at zero after a week or two.** Ads cost real Robux and need the person's own
    account: ask before any spend. Plays objective, no targeting, 5 credits a day for 3 days, lifetime budget, fixed
    end date, auto-reload off, three thumbnails, no pausing. **The person, 20-30 min:** decides, buys 15 credits
    (about 3,945 Robux at the 2025 rate: check the form), starts it. Done when: plays per credit for each thumbnail
    is read two days after it ends. Low trust: only gaps of several times count.
11. **Free placements.** **The person, 15-20 min:** Standout Games form, if the mechanic or look is new. One
    event per real update, made 7 days ahead with featuring ticked, only after the build passes. One
    60-character announcement, at most one every 3 days. Done when: the event and announcement are set.
12. **Weekly read.** Pull numbers for players who came from Home only: play-through rate, bounce under 60 s and
    at 61-180 s, play days for Day 1, 2-7 and 8-28, playtime. Ignore Home dips during ad runs. Tiny samples: low
    trust. Done when: the weakest number against the dashboard's P10/P50/P90 is named as the next build job.
13. **Update rhythm.** Every 2-4 weeks, one change, with a scripted event that runs itself at a fixed weekend
    hour and a claim window of days. **The person** publishes it and reposts the gameplay cut on one outside channel
    they pick, with its own share link. Low to medium confidence. Done when: the update and its event are out.

## Never
- Publish, pay or set a price: the person only. No money moves without their yes in chat, and Robux
  turned into ad credit never come back. Never ask for an API key in chat, or download without asking.
- Put a name, picture, text, event or ad live that the person has not picked, or pick a look yourself. They pick from
  real versions, each picture sent itself, one at a time. Picture work is /make-model's, in a model chat they start.
  Any paid picture or video tool needs the person's own account, costs credits, and is asked about first.
- Run a step past 2 before the grey-box gate passes, skip the tester watch, or count scripts as testers. Games with no
  fun gate tend to grow big, turn out boring and don't launch, and a skipped playtest hides it.
- Judge the game on blended numbers: ad players never count in ranking and behave worse.
- Buy bots, visits, likes or "CCU boosts": Roblox excludes them and can suspend the account.
- Use "FREE", Robux or giveaway wording, lookalike names, or thumbnails showing what the game lacks:
  exposure is cut and recovery takes days to weeks.
- Put a Discord invite, handle, URL or QR code in the game: only the page's Social Links are allowed.
- Run contests with Robux or cash prizes (banned), or sell random items for Robux in version one (they need
  exact odds and a country check).
- Leave an ad on "run continuously" (one developer lost 75,744 Robux that way), or use Engagement or Earnings
  objectives, expedited review (50,000 Robux), the $5,000 tile or paid creators: those are studio budgets.
- Follow notes about QPTR, a 7-day window or keyword search ads (all retired), or write forum figures such as
  "50-100 CCU" down as rules: no staff confirmed any.
- Build on someone else's meme character or brand: one hit had to pull a character.

## Proof so far
- Researched 2026-11-15: Roblox's own docs and staff posts, read through a summarising tool, two checkers per claim.
- Not found: a solo launch after June 2026 with numbers, or whether Home recommends a game in the 16+ trial.
- Nothing here has been run on a game yet, until "What we tried" says otherwise.
- After every use, add one dated line to "What we tried" in `REFERENCE.md`: what was done, on which game,
  what happened. Lines are only added. A step that fails twice gets fixed or cut.

## Hand over
- In: the idea card from /game-idea (step 2 adds the rating aim and clip moment), then a build past the grey box.
- Out: steps 3 and 4, then each week's weakest number with its Home numbers: the next job, one change per update.
  Each goes in `Work/<Game>/Plan.md` as a step (What, Done when, Test, the person checks) with their yes.
  /build-game builds game and code, /game-ui the first minutes and screens, /make-model any picture in a model chat
  the person starts.

## Done when
The step asked for meets its own "Done when", the person said yes in chat to anything that went live or cost
money, the next job is named, and "What we tried" has a new dated line.
