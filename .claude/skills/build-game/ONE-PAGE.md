## Gates

| Gate | What it proves | Who passes it | Passed on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Grey box: core loop only | The person wants another go | The person: plays about 15 min, four questions | |
| 2 Real players, 16+ (no fee) | D1 and session time reach the middle of the band, Home players | Players' numbers; the person reviews | |
| 3 Progression | D7 is inside the band | Players' numbers; the person reviews | |
| 4 Money and all ages | Starter pack, passes, the fee, 250 highly engaged plays in 60 days | The person pays, prices; Roblox counts | |

Gate 1 questions: want another go? a choice that mattered? a reason to return tomorrow? the hook in one line?
Pass: yes to "want another go" and at most one "no" in all. A fail: back to /game-idea, not more building.
Before Gate 2: a phone run, and 2-3 friends watched for 15 minutes each. D1, D7: back the next day, a week later.
Band: the range Roblox's dashboard shows for similar games (50th to 90th line); middle is halfway between.
250 and the fee are from 2026-11-15: check the Audience Reach tab and the Publish page.
